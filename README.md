Procurify is an intelligent spend management and procure-to-pay platform that provides organizations with unprecedented visibility and control over business spend. The platform offers a RESTful API that enables developers to integrate procurement workflows — including purchase requests, approvals, purchase orders, vendor management, and budget tracking — into their own systems and processes. Authentication is handled via OAuth 2.0 client credentials flow, with tokens scoped to the requesting domain and user. The API supports core spend management resources such as requisitions, purchase orders, accounts payable, receipts, account codes, vendors, and catalog items, all returning and consuming JSON. Procurify does not support webhooks; all integrations are pull-based through the REST endpoints documented at developer.procurify.com.

**Areas:** Spend Management · Procurement · Purchase Orders · Accounts Payable · Vendor Management · Budget Tracking · Procure-to-Pay · ERP Integration
